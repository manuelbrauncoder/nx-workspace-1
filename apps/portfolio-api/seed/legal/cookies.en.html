This website does without tracking. It only sets cookies and storage entries it needs to work, and only once you change a setting or sign in. No consent is required for this (§ 25 (2) TDDDG), which is why there is no cookie banner.

What is stored

  • Cookie “lang”: remembers the chosen language. Set when you switch the language, expires after one year.
  • Color scheme in local storage: remembers whether you chose the light or the dark color scheme. Stays in your browser and is not sent to the server.
  • Cookie “sid”: keeps you signed in. Set only when you sign in, not readable by scripts, expires after seven days or when you sign out.

What is not stored

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party cookies.

Deleting cookies

You can delete cookies and local storage at any time in the settings of your browser. The website then falls back to the default language and the color scheme of your system.
