Hosting and server logs

vyte runs on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. When a page is requested, the server processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time, requested address and browser identification to deliver the page and keep the service secure. The logs are deleted after [enter retention period]. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

Host accounts

Accounts are only created through an invite link. vyte stores the email address, optionally a name, and the password as a hash, never in plain text. The data is used to sign in and to work on events together. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. When an account is deleted, its own events with their guests, images and photos are deleted as well.

Events, guest lists and answers

Hosts create events and add their guests: name, number of possible companions and a note only the hosts see. Every guest gets a personal link. Through it vyte stores the answer, the number of companions and an optional message to the hosts. Only the hosts of the event see this data. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR, the legitimate interest of the hosts in planning their event. The guest list and the answers are deleted with the event.

Photos of guests

Guests can upload photos through their photo link or the photo stand of an event, at the stand optionally with a name. The photos are stored without metadata (e.g. location and camera) and shown only to the hosts of the event and to the device or guest who uploaded them. Own photos can be deleted again. The photos are deleted with the event. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, uploading is voluntary.

Cookies and local storage

vyte only sets technically necessary cookies: for signing in, for the photo stand and for the chosen language. The color scheme is kept in the local storage of the browser. No consent is required for this (§ 25 (2) TDDDG). All details are in the cookie policy.

Fonts and external content

Fonts, scripts and images are delivered by the own server. No third-party content is embedded. A link “Open in map” leads to OpenStreetMap only when you click it.

Analytics and tracking

vyte uses no analytics or tracking services.

Contact by email

If you send an email, your details are processed to answer the request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The message is deleted once it is no longer needed.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, the right to object to the processing and to withdraw a consent. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
