vyte does without tracking. The app only sets cookies and storage entries it needs to work, and only once you change a setting, sign in or upload a photo at a photo stand. No consent is required for this (§ 25 (2) TDDDG), which is why there is no cookie banner.

What is stored

  • Cookie “__Host-sid”: keeps you signed in. Set only when you sign in, not readable by scripts, expires after seven days or when you sign out.
  • Cookie “vyte_stand_device”: recognizes your device at the photo stand of an event, so you see your own photos and the limit per device applies. Set at your first upload at a photo stand, not readable by scripts, only sent to the photo stand, expires after 30 days.
  • Cookie “lang”: remembers the chosen language. Set when you switch the language, expires after one year.
  • Color scheme in local storage: remembers whether you chose the light or the dark color scheme. Stays in your browser and is not sent to the server.

What is not stored

There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies and no third-party cookies.

Deleting cookies

You can delete cookies and local storage at any time in the settings of your browser. You are then signed out, your device counts as new at a photo stand, and the app uses the default language and the color scheme of your system again.
